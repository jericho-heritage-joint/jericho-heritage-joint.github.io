Red Pandadada: Idle RPG

Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

Jihyeong Jeon (the “Developer”), the developer of “Red Pandadada: Idle RPG” (the “Game”), complies with the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea and other applicable laws and regulations, and processes the personal information of people who use the Game in accordance with this policy.

At a glance
  • We do not collect contact details such as your name, phone number or email address.
  • Your game progress is saved to the cloud so you can continue on a new device.
  • We collect play statistics to improve the Game; you can turn this off in Settings → Account.
  • The Game has rewarded ads, and tracking for personalized ads happens only if you allow it.
  • You can delete your account and information at any time via Settings → Account → Delete Account.

1. Personal information we process and why

You enter your nickname yourself; everything else is generated or transmitted automatically while you use the Game.

① Account and game saves

Items
Unity player ID, Game Center player ID (if you are signed in to Game Center), nickname and adventurer number, game progress information (rounds, currencies, items, etc.)
Purpose
Verifying your account, saving game progress and loading it on a new device, preventing fraudulent use
Retention period
Until you delete your account

② Features you use with other players

Items
Nickname, Forest Card (game information such as title, rank, Power, costumes and village buildings), leaderboard scores, guild name and guild chat (only phrases preset by the Game), Co-op Hunt participation information, items listed on the Auction House
Purpose
Providing leaderboards, guilds, neighbor villages and likes, Co-op Hunts and the Auction House
Visibility
Your nickname and the game information above are visible to other players.
Retention period
Until you delete your account (weekly rankings are reset every Monday)

③ Payments

Items
Products purchased, App Store transaction IDs, delivery history
Purpose
Delivering purchased products, preventing duplicate delivery, restoring purchases
Note
Payments are processed by Apple. The Developer does not receive payment method information such as card numbers, and payment records are kept by Apple in accordance with the relevant laws and its own policies.
Retention period
Until you delete your account

④ Play statistics (Unity Analytics)

Items
Unity player ID and installation ID, device information (model, operating system, language, etc.), approximate location such as country (estimated from your IP address), usage records (sessions and play time, progress stage, payment and ad-viewing records), performance and error information
Purpose
Improving the Game and adjusting its balance, checking errors, usage statistics
How to opt out
If you turn off Settings → Account → ‘Send Play Stats’, we stop collecting from that moment. On devices located in the European Union, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or China, and on devices whose region cannot be determined, we ask for consent first and collect only if you consent.
Retention period
Destroyed once the purpose has been achieved, and never kept beyond the retention period set by Unity. When you delete your account, we ask Unity to delete it.

⑤ Ads (LevelPlay, Unity Ads, ironSource Ads)

Items
Advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), identifier for vendor (IDFV), IP address, device information, ad viewing and click records
Purpose
Showing rewarded ads and granting rewards, measuring ad performance and preventing fraud, and personalized ads if you allow tracking
How it is collected
The ad companies collect it directly through the ad SDKs included in the Game. It may be transmitted even when you are not watching an ad, so that ads can be loaded in advance.
How to opt out
You can decline when asked whether to allow tracking at the start of the Game, or turn it off in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. You can also opt out of personalized ads via the privacy icon on Unity Ads ad screens. Even if you decline tracking, you can use the Game and ad rewards just the same.
Retention period
Governed by each ad company’s privacy policy.

⑥ Other automatically generated information

While you use online features, access records such as your IP address and access time may be kept on the server. Game notifications are scheduled only on your device, and no information is sent for notifications.

2. Destruction of personal information

  • When you tap Settings → Account → Delete Account, we delete without delay your cloud save, Unity account, Forest Card, guild and Co-op Hunt participation and Auction House items, and also delete the save on this device. For play statistics, we ask Unity to delete them.
  • If you only delete the app, the information in the cloud remains. If you signed in with Game Center, you can reinstall the app, sign in with the same account and then delete your account; otherwise, please send a request to the contact in Section 9.
  • Electronic files are deleted using methods that make them unrecoverable.
  • Information that must be kept under other laws is stored separately for the required period and then destroyed.

3. Provision of personal information to third parties

The Developer does not provide personal information to third parties, with the following exceptions:

  • Your nickname and the game information in 1-② are visible to other players as part of the Game’s features.
  • Ad companies directly collect the information in 1-⑤ through the ad SDKs included in the Game.
  • When requested by investigative agencies or others in accordance with the law

5. Rights of users and their legal guardians

  • You may at any time request access to, correction of, deletion of, or suspension of processing of your personal information.
  • What you can do directly in the Game: delete your account and turn off play statistics (Settings → Account), and turn off tracking (iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking)
  • Please send any other requests to the contact in Section 9. If you include the nickname and adventurer number shown in Settings → Account, we will verify them and process your request without delay.
  • Legal guardians of children under the age of 14 may also exercise the same rights.

6. Children under the age of 14

The Game does not collect information that can directly identify a child, such as a name or contact details. If we learn that personal information of a child under the age of 14, which requires the consent of a legal guardian, has been processed without such consent, we will destroy it without delay.

7. Security measures

  • Communication between the Game and the servers is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Information stored on the servers can be read and written only by the signed-in account owner, and other players see only the items the Game has defined.
  • Only the Developer uses the server administration screens.
  • We process only the minimum information the Game needs.

8. Automatic collection tools

The Game does not use cookies. The advertising identifier is used only if you allow tracking, as described in 1-⑤. This page is operated on GitHub Pages, and GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes.
