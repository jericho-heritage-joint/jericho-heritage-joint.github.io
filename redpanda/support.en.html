Payments

I paid, but I didn’t get my item.

If the game closes before the payment finishes, the item is delivered automatically the next time you open the game. Try closing the game completely and opening it again. If it still hasn’t arrived, please contact us with the order ID from your Apple receipt.

It says ‘Waiting for parent/guardian approval’.

If Family Sharing’s ‘Ask to Buy’ is on, a parent or guardian has to approve the payment. Once it’s approved, you get it in the game right away.

It says ‘Purchases are restricted on this device’.

Please check that ‘In-app Purchases’ is allowed under iPhone Settings → Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions → iTunes & App Store Purchases.

I’d like a refund (cancellation).

  • Refunds for items purchased on the App Store are handled by Apple. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Within 7 days of purchase, if you haven’t used any of the delivered items at all, you can request a cancellation.
  • Cancellation is restricted for items that have been used even in part, and for items whose time-based benefits have already started (Forest Pass, Monthly Golden Apples, Remove Ads).
  • If a refund is made, the delivered items are taken back.
  • If a minor paid without the consent of a legal guardian, the minor or the legal guardian can request a cancellation.

Where can I see the odds of randomized items?

You can find them under ‘Drop Rates’ at the bottom of the Shop in the game.

Restoring purchases

I changed devices and Remove Ads isn’t working.

On a device signed in with the same Apple ID, it usually comes back automatically. If it doesn’t, tap ‘Restore Purchases’ at the bottom of the Shop.

Currencies such as Golden Apples and items you got from packs are carried over by your account’s cloud save, not by Restore Purchases (see ‘Account and saves’ below).

Account and saves

I want to continue on a new device.

If you play while signed in to Game Center, your progress is saved to the cloud. On the new device, sign in with the same Game Center account and open the game, and it will ask whether to load your progress from the cloud. Tap ‘Load’.

I played without Game Center.

If you aren’t signed in to Game Center, your account exists only on that device and can’t be moved to another device. Before changing devices, sign in to Game Center in iPhone Settings and then open the game. Your progress on this device will be saved to that account.

When does the cloud save happen?

Every few minutes when your progress changes, and when you leave the game. In Settings → Account you can see the time of the last save and tap ‘Save Now’.

I want to delete my account.

Tap Settings → Account → Delete Account. Your progress in the cloud and the save on this device are all deleted, and this can’t be undone. If you only delete the app, the information in the cloud remains. The information that gets deleted is listed in the Privacy Policy.

Ads

I tapped an ad reward button, but no ad plays.

The ad may still be loading, or there may be no ad to show right now. Please check your internet connection and try again in a moment.

What is ‘Remove Ads’?

It lets you get ad rewards right away without watching ads, plus 50 Golden Apples every day. Buy it once and it stays on.

I want to turn off personalized ads.

You can turn them off in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Even with it off, you can use the game and ad rewards just the same.

Privacy and notifications

I don’t want to send play statistics.

Turn off Settings → Account → ‘Send Play Stats’, and nothing will be sent from then on.

I want to turn off notifications.

You can turn them off in iPhone Settings → Notifications → Red Pandadada.
